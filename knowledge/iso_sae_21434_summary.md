# ISO/SAE 21434 (road vehicle cybersecurity engineering) - concepts (original summary)

- Cybersecurity lifecycle: concept, product development, validation, production, operations and maintenance, decommissioning.
- TARA (Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment): identify assets, damage scenarios, threat scenarios, attack paths, feasibility and risk value; assign cybersecurity goals (CAL levels).
- Cybersecurity monitoring and incident response during operation, including vulnerability triage and field update process.
- Continual activities: cybersecurity case, assurance, supplier management.
- Traceability between goals, requirements, design, tests and residual risk decisions.
- For mobile robots: treat actuation commands, localization sensors and update channels as high-value assets.
